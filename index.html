YOUR CREW: 2–4 NEIGHBOURS

Three routes.
One simple check code.

Everyone writes all three calculations.

Swap who checks after each route.

1 Find distances A, B and C in metres.

2 Add those three answers.

3 Enter the total to approve the routes.

No joining digits.
No extra conversion.
The sum is the code.
